# Stage 10 kickoff

Stage 10 starts directly from the validated Stage 9 main baseline.

Baseline commit: `a18a469722377eb797152b48a8f152768673c1ae`

The Stage 9 baseline is frozen before Stage 10 implementation begins.

## Working-tree contract

The local checkout must be on `main`, synchronized with `origin/main`, and have no tracked or untracked implementation changes before the first Stage 10 commit. Development environment directories such as `.venv/` must remain ignored/untracked.

## First-commit workflow

1. Synchronize and verify `main`.
2. Verify the baseline tag points to the Stage 9 freeze commit.
3. Run the full baseline test suite.
4. Create the Stage 10 convention/specification change as the first implementation commit.
5. Run focused Stage 10 tests, then the complete matrix before advancing to the next Stage 10 step.

No Stage 8 numerical/API contract may be changed implicitly by Stage 10 kickoff work.
