# Stage 12.6 — Candidate Manifest

Candidate version: `0.3.0rc1`

The immutable candidate commit is the commit at the tip of the `release/0.3.0-rc1` branch after all Stage 12.6 version, metadata, documentation, test, and workflow changes have landed.

## Artifacts

The release-candidate workflow generates exactly:

- `stochx-0.3.0rc1.tar.gz`
- `stochx-0.3.0rc1-<python-tag>-<abi-tag>-<platform-tag>.whl`
- `SHA256SUMS.txt`

The workflow artifact bundle is the authoritative candidate artifact set.

## Hash policy

SHA-256 values are generated from the exact bytes produced by `python -m build` on the candidate commit. No locally rebuilt file may be substituted for the CI artifact when recording the candidate hashes.

## Verification policy

The candidate is valid only when every required CI job passes against the same candidate commit SHA.
